LUMÉRA

A premium Hero Ava hair-care commercial built around beauty ritual, product care, a polished hair reveal and a refined spa-luxury world.

15-second commercialHero AvaBeauty storytelling
01 — Project Brief

More than hair care.

LUMÉRA uses Ava as the direct-to-camera beauty spokesperson, moving the audience through care, transformation and a confident finished look.

The campaign world is intentionally soft and premium: cream and ivory styling, stone, marble, greenery and warm beauty light.

02 — Visual Sequence

Care → reveal → confidence.

The supplied reference pack locks Ava's beauty look, the LUMÉRA product system, the modest washing scene, the finished hair reveal and the final product hero.

03 — Creative Direction

Beauty that feels considered.

The campaign balances aspiration with an approachable spokesperson-led delivery, keeping the product visible without making the film feel like a catalogue.

01

Hero Ava

Ava speaks directly to camera, giving the beauty proposition a clear human voice.

02

Wet hair, shampoo foam, satin and warm stone create tactile beauty cues.

03

The finished hair reveal provides the emotional payoff before the product close.

06 — What This Demonstrates

Building a complete beauty story in 15 seconds.

LUMÉRA demonstrates how a single Hero Ava can carry beauty messaging through direct address, ritual, transformation and a product-led close.

The system is adaptable to shampoo, skincare, fragrance and other premium consumer beauty launches.
