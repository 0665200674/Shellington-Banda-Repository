Beauty / UGC

Make the creator feel real.
A social-first beauty concept built around natural expression, skincare ritual and intimate close-up imagery—designed to feel native to creator-led content.

Role — Creative direction & AI production
Format — UGC / beauty social concept
Focus — Beauty · skincare · creator content
Status — Portfolio concept
Selected sequence from the beauty creator-content concept.
01 — The Brief

Create beauty content that feels native, not over-produced.

The creative objective was to make a beauty/skincare visual feel personal and believable—closer to the way a creator would demonstrate a product than to a traditional studio commercial.

The sequence uses a home-like setting, direct-to-camera moments, product interaction and close facial detail to build trust and immediacy.

02 — The Concept

From skincare ritual to social story.

The visual language is intentionally intimate: natural daylight, relaxed wardrobe, close framing and simple gestures. Product interaction gives the viewer a reason to stay engaged while the creator remains the human centre of the piece.

03 — The Process

Designing for authenticity.

01

Creator Direction

Define the persona, setting, tone and natural behaviours that make the content feel socially native.

02

Visual Identity

Establish wardrobe, environment, lighting and beauty details that support a credible creator world.

03

Generation

Develop the creator imagery and motion with attention to facial detail, hands, product interaction and expression.

04

Continuity

Keep the creator, environment and visual language coherent enough for a sequence rather than isolated shots.

05

Editing

Shape the footage into a short-form rhythm built for attention, clarity and natural creator pacing.

06

Social Adaptation

Extend the core concept into potential Reels, TikTok, Shorts, paid-social and product-demo variations.
