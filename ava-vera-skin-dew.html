AVA VERA SKIN DEW

A Hero Ava skin-care commercial built around beauty, direct address and a polished skin-care advertising world.

Hero AvaSkin careBeauty advertising
01 — Project Brief

Make skin care feel effortless.

AVA VERA SKIN DEW uses Hero Ava as the visible face of the campaign, placing the spokesperson and the skin-care proposition in the same visual world.

The case study keeps the supplied commercial at the centre and avoids adding product claims, ingredients or performance promises that are not established by the supplied asset.

02 — Creative Direction

Glow, intimacy, confidence.

A clean beauty treatment designed to feel premium and human, with Ava carrying the communication and the product remaining commercially visible.

01 — HERO AVA

Recognisable spokesperson.

Ava creates continuity across the Hero Ava campaign family and gives the brand a consistent human presence.

02 — SKIN CARE

Beauty-first presentation.

The visual approach prioritises polished beauty imagery and an intimate consumer-care atmosphere.

03 — PRODUCT

Commercial clarity.

The film is structured as advertising, keeping the skin-care product proposition connected to the hero rather than isolated from her.

03 — The Film

The finished VERA Skin Dew commercial.

The supplied AVA VERA SKIN DEW film is presented directly as the primary campaign asset.
