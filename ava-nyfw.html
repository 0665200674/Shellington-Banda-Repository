AVA / NYFW

Style an arrival that feels editorial.
A high-end fashion concept built around AVA as a digital fashion personality, developed as a three-part NYFW visual sequence spanning arrival, Day 2 and Day 3 looks.

Role — Creative direction & AI production
Format — Fashion campaign / social video
Focus — AI fashion · character styling · editorial motion
Status — Portfolio concept
AVA — High-End NYFW Arrival. The opening piece in the visual sequence.
01 — The Brief

Build a fashion world around one recognisable AI character.

The objective was to create a premium fashion narrative that could live across short-form video and still imagery while keeping AVA visually central.

Rather than treating each generation as an isolated image, the work is structured like a small editorial run: a named character, distinct looks and multiple moments that can be released as a sequence.

02 — The Visual Sequence

Three looks. One fashion identity.

The repository contains three AVA video pieces, plus supporting stills for the arrival, Day 2 and a Day 3 black-corset / tailored-trouser look.
