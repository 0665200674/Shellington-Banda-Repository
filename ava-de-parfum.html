AI Advertising · Fragrance Campaign

AVA de Parfum.

A two-fragrance visual campaign built around AVA ROSE BLUSH and AVA SOLARA SUNLIT—using AI-assisted image and video production to give each scent its own visual identity.

AVA ROSE BLUSHFragrance film · Eau de Parfum
AVA SOLARA SUNLITFragrance film · Eau de Parfum
01 — The Campaign

One fragrance house. Two distinct moods.

AVA de Parfum is presented as a paired campaign rather than a single generic product spot. The creative system gives each fragrance a dedicated film and product still while keeping the AVA identity coherent across the work.

The emphasis is on premium product presentation, atmosphere and short-form visual storytelling—turning fragrance into a visual world rather than relying on product placement alone.

02 — Product Worlds

The stills establish the visual language.

The two supplied product images act as campaign anchors: clear product references that support the films and make each fragrance immediately identifiable.

Rose BlushProduct reference / campaign still
Solara SunlitProduct reference / campaign still
03 — The Visual Sequence

From product identity to moving image.

Each fragrance receives its own video treatment. Together, the two films demonstrate how a shared campaign architecture can support different visual expressions without losing product focus.

Rose Blush

Soft, elevated fragrance storytelling.

The Rose Blush film is presented as a focused luxury product story, with the bottle and campaign imagery acting as the visual reference point.

Solara Sunlit

Bright, confident product energy.

The Solara Sunlit film extends the campaign into a second fragrance world, using movement and cinematic presentation to distinguish the product from its companion.

04 — Creative Direction

Designing two identities inside one campaign.

The creative approach is deliberately modular: establish the product, build an atmosphere around it, create motion, then finish with a clear product-led moment. This makes the work suitable for campaign films as well as shorter social cutdowns.

01

AVA ROSE BLUSH

Positioned as the softer, more romantic expression within the pair. The visual treatment prioritises elegance, beauty and an intimate premium feel.

02

AVA SOLARA SUNLIT

Positioned as the brighter, more radiant expression. The visual treatment gives the campaign a second energy while retaining the same product-first discipline.

05 — Production Process

Built as a repeatable AI advertising workflow.

The campaign is structured around a practical sequence that can be repeated for additional fragrances, formats and social placements.

01

02

Creative world

Develop the visual mood and storytelling direction for each fragrance rather than treating both as identical executions.

03

04

05

07 — What This Demonstrates

Premium product storytelling without a conventional production footprint.

This project demonstrates the ability to take a small set of product references and develop a coherent advertising system around them: distinct creative identities, product-led stills, cinematic video, and a repeatable structure for future fragrance variants.
