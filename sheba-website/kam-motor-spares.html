Case Study / 01

KAM Motor
Spares.

Visibility & Sales Campaign — a coordinated brand, advertising and content campaign designed to make a trusted local motor-spares business more visible and drive demand across its suspension and undercar range.

Petny Enterprises t/a KAM Motor Spares2025
Creative & Advertising
Campaign · Print · Social · Video
The challenge

Good products.
Not enough visibility.

KAM Motor Spares had strong product quality and trusted brands on its shelves, but limited visibility against larger, more established competitors in its local market.

The opportunity was to turn product credibility into a clearer market presence — giving customers a simple reason to notice KAM, trust what it offered and make an enquiry or visit.

ClientKAM Motor Spares
CategoryMotor spares
FocusSuspension
The work

Built for the road.
Not the showroom.

SHEBA developed a coordinated campaign system across a wide-format promotional banner, matching print/social flyer and companion video ad. The creative connected the product range, trusted brands and practical customer benefits under one consistent message.

Campaign system

One idea.
Multiple touchpoints.

01 / MESSAGE

Make the proposition memorable.

“Built for the road. Not the showroom.” gave the campaign a clear, practical point of view aligned to the customer’s real need.

02 / TRUST

Use proof where it matters.

Trust-building cues and recognised product brands — including RBI, 555, EEP and KYB — were integrated into the visual system.

03 / REACH

Design once. Extend intelligently.

The same visual language was adapted across physical signage, print/social communication and video to create a consistent campaign presence.

04 / ACTION

Make the next step obvious.

Opening hours, location and contact details were given prominent treatment so awareness could translate into enquiries and visits.

Outcome

Visibility that
started moving.

Client-reported outcome

KAM Motor Spares reported increased visibility in its local market and improved sales, with growing demand across its suspension-parts range following the campaign.

Outcome supplied by the client; specific sales figures and market-share data were not provided, so no numerical performance claim is made here.

SHEBA's role

Creative built around
a business objective.

The work wasn't treated as a standalone flyer. It was built as a connected visibility and sales campaign.

Strategy, message, visual direction, campaign design and channel adaptation were brought together so the brand could show up consistently wherever customers encountered it.
