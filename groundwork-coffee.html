Groundwork Coffee

Turn a coffee brand into a host-led story.
A podcast advertising concept built around a human presenter, conversational delivery and a visual language designed to make branded messaging feel native to the show.

Role — Creative direction & AI production
Format — Podcast / branded video ad
Focus — Coffee · host-led storytelling · advertising
Status — Portfolio concept
Groundwork Coffee podcast advertising concept. The repository currently contains the finished ad video and the host reference image used to support the visual concept.
01 — The Brief

Make branded audio-led advertising feel human.

The concept explores how a coffee brand can enter a podcast environment without feeling like a conventional interruption. The host becomes the bridge between product and audience, giving the message a personal, conversational frame.

The creative direction is built around clarity, personality and a repeatable host-led format that can be adapted across episodes and social cut-downs.

02 — The Visual System

One recognisable host. One repeatable advertising format.

The host reference provides a visual anchor for the production system. Rather than treating the presenter as a one-off generated image, the concept establishes a consistent on-camera identity that can carry branded messaging across multiple pieces of content.

03 — The Production Process

Built for repeatable content.

01

Brand & Audience

Define the coffee brand message, audience context and role the podcast placement should play.

02

Host Direction

Establish presenter identity, framing, tone and delivery so the ad feels like content rather than a generic commercial.

03

Script & Structure

Shape the message around a concise opening, product value and clear brand recall without overloading the viewer.

04

AI Production

Use AI-assisted image, video and voice workflows to build the host-led visual and accelerate iteration.

05

Edit & Sound

Combine performance, pacing, sound and visual transitions into a short advertising sequence.

06

Content Extensions

Adapt the master idea into podcast placements, vertical social clips, paid ads and short branded segments.

AI advertising with a format-first mindset.

This project demonstrates the ability to translate a brand message into a host-led advertising format, combine AI-assisted production with commercial storytelling, and design a concept that can be repurposed beyond a single master asset.
